The birth of this application comes with a story—and in the following section, I’ll be sharing that story with you.

#story
As someone with color blindness, I’ve faced unique challenges in my role providing after-sales service for X-ray imaging equipment. Early in my career, I encountered teasing and mockery due to my condition, which made certain tasks more difficult. Determined to address this with a practical and cost-effective solution, I came up with an idea: create a simple, accessible application that can identify colors from images.

The concept was straightforward—take a photo, click on any point, and have the app tell me the true color of that area. While I may struggle to distinguish colors visually, I can read their names and use that information to differentiate them in my work. This inspired me to join GitHub and create a dedicated space to build a clean, functional app to help mitigate the challenges of my color blindness.

This project isn’t just about code; it’s about empowerment. By developing this tool, I aim to reduce the difficulties I face and minimize the judgment or teasing from others in my workplace. I hope to share this app with others who might benefit from it and invite the community to contribute ideas or improvements to make it even better.
